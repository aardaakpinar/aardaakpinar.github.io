For a long time, I tried to share everything through YouTube. I liked the idea of it: talking through a project, showing how something works, putting a face to the work. But forcing every idea into a video format made the whole process much heavier than it needed to be. Between recording, filming, and editing, even a small thought could take days to publish.

Eventually I had to admit something to myself: the format was getting in the way of the idea. That is exactly why I started this blog. I wanted a place where I could quickly turn whatever is on my mind into writing, in whatever form fits that day.

## What will you find here?

- Notes about the open-source projects I am working on
- Things I am learning in cybersecurity
- Small technical tips and code snippets
- And probably a few things I have not thought of yet

I am not trying to turn this into a polished publication. Think of it more as my workspace, with the door left open. Some posts will be careful guides, others will be closer to notes I am writing for my future self.

## Did I quit YouTube?

Technically, yes. At least for now.

The honest reason is time. Making videos takes a lot of it, and I would rather spend that time building things, breaking them, and writing about what I learn along the way. That said, I am not ruling out making videos from time to time. If an idea really wants to be a video, I will probably make one.

For now, though, this is where I will be writing. It feels good to have a place that is mine again.
