For a while, I’ve been trying to share content with you through YouTube videos. But forcing every idea into a video format makes the whole process much more complicated than it needs to be. Between recording, filming, and editing, even bringing a small idea to life can take days.

That’s exactly why I started this blog: I wanted a place where I could quickly turn whatever is on my mind into writing, in whatever format feels most appropriate.

## What will you find here?

- Notes about the open-source projects I’m working on
- Things I’ve learned in cybersecurity
- Small technical tips and code snippets
- And many other things I haven’t thought of yet.

## Did I quit YouTube?

Technically, yes. At least for now.

I made this decision mainly because making videos takes up a lot of my time. That said, I’m not ruling out making videos from time to time. You might still see a new one every now and then.
